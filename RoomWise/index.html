BEFORE YOU PREDICT

Input units guide

Temperature · °C

Enter the air temperature in Celsius, not Fahrenheit.

Humidity · %

Enter relative humidity as a percentage, such as 35 for 35%.

Light · lux

Use a light sensor's illuminance reading, not a bulb's wattage.

CO₂ · ppm

Use the carbon dioxide reading in parts per million.

Take readings from the same room at the same time. You can also use the recorded examples for practice.
